What is this?

A second human checks whether one candidate record respects the visible Human Mesh rules.

2

Why another human?

Because CI can prepare and validate structure, but it must not silently become the final authority over a person.

3

What power?

Only approve, pause, or reject the current record, with a short reason. Review does not publish.

4

What boundary?

No person scoring, moral labels, inferred motives, hidden veto, or copying unnecessary private information.
