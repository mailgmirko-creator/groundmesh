GroundMesh Human Mesh · H0 Foundation

Every human may connect. No human is compelled to connect.

The Human Mesh is the long-range GroundMesh foundation for voluntary planetary presence: a way for people to declare themselves, find one another, make chosen responsibility visible, correct themselves in public when needed, and leave without becoming a score in somebody else's machine.

Foundation only — global registration is not open yet

This page defines the architecture we intend to grow into. Broad planetary intake remains closed under ADR-0005 until moderation, privacy, consent, correction/removal, operations, and rollback are proven through smaller pilots.

The inversion we are aiming for

GroundMesh should make hidden power and public stewardship easier to inspect without making private human life easier to harvest.

Make voluntary responsibility easier to see; never make human worth computable.

1. Public declaration plane

A minimal public record: stable GroundMesh node id, public name or stable pseudonym, country/region/city-level location, optional statement, explicit consent, and lifecycle state.

No passport, government ID, home address, private contact details, biometrics, birth date, hidden profile, or inferred sensitive traits.

2. Private enrollment plane

If stronger continuity or verification is ever needed, it belongs in a separate hardened service—not in this static public site or public ledger. Verification must be proportional to the task and must not become a worth hierarchy.

3. Open Accountability Log

Instead of a system deciding who is "light" or "dark," a participant may voluntarily publish self-authored commitments, relevant conflict disclosures, corrections, stewardship actions, withdrawals, and restorations.

The system preserves provenance. It does not calculate virtue.

4. Right to correct and leave

Participation is not a trap. Records are versioned. Correction and withdrawal are first-class lifecycle actions. Pausing participation must not become a shame label or loss of dignity.

What "everyone" means here

The invitation is universal, not compulsory. GroundMesh may eventually make registration easy enough that any person on Earth can declare presence, but browsing the site, appearing in public records, or being imported from another dataset can never count as consent.

GroundMesh must never scrape people into the Human Mesh.

Identity continuity without mandatory legal names

A participant may use a public name or a stable pseudonym. The requirement is continuity: one declared identity should not become a disposable mask for evading accountability or manipulating decisions. Legal-name verification, if ever needed for a specific role, belongs to the separate private plane.

Location without unnecessary exposure

The public contract only allows country, region, or city precision. Exact residence coordinates and home addresses do not belong in the Human Mesh public record.
