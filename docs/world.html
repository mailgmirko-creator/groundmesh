GroundMesh World · public v0.1

See needs. See capacity. Act freely.

GroundMesh is building a public coordination layer where evidence-backed needs and available capacity can become easier to see, connect, verify, and correct — without turning the platform into a central planner or a judge of people.

No account is required to read this page. The numbers below are dated global indicators, not live local requests and not a score of the world.

Coordination, not control

The loop GroundMesh is testing

Need→ Capacity→ Possible match→ Consent→ Commitment→ Delivery→ Verification→ Correction

A proposal is not a command. A CI suggestion is not consent. A commitment is not delivery. A number without unit, place, time window, quality threshold, provenance, and uncertainty is not yet a coordination fact.

Current boundary

What is not live yet

  • No open public feed of real needs and offers yet.
  • No autonomous resource allocation or compulsory matching.
  • No emergency-response promise.
  • No person-worth, moral, or reputation score.
  • No claim that these global indicators tell us what any specific locality needs.

The next real-world coordination demonstration should use low-risk public data and remain read-only until evidence, consent, privacy, correction, and governance rules are strong enough for more.
