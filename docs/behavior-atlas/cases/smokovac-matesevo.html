Current pattern

Distribution of benefits and burdens: mixed / emerging. The record contains a development/connectivity rationale and an IMF-projected macroeconomic benefit, while also documenting substantial sovereign fiscal burden and opportunity costs.

Resource stewardship: mixed / emerging. The project addressed a stated infrastructure objective, but IMF records associate phase 1 with high public debt and uncertain returns relative to other uses of scarce resources.

Evidence trail

1. Government development rationale

In 2016, the Government of Montenegro publicly framed motorway construction as a development-policy precondition for reducing northern underdevelopment and enabling additional economic activity.

“the most important and most valuable precondition to duly and permanently overcome the problems of the underdevelopment of the northern areas”

Government of Montenegro · 11 Oct 2016

Check the Government source

2. Priority section entered service

The Government announced that the Smokovac–Mateševo priority section would officially enter use on 14 July 2022.

“The priority section of the motorway will officially be in use from 14 July at 8:00 a.m.”

Government of Montenegro · 12 Jul 2022

Check the opening notice

3. IMF positive macroeconomic projection

IMF staff estimated that the highway would lift 2023 GDP relative to a no-highway counterfactual, while noting low traffic projections reduced the expected supply-side boost.

“total GDP in 2023 will be about 3 p.p. of 2014 GDP higher with the highway than without it”

IMF Country Report No. 18/121

Check the IMF country report

4. IMF debt-burden evidence

The IMF's 2018 Article IV assessment said construction of the first Bar–Boljare section played a key role in raising Montenegro's government debt to high levels.

“the construction of the first section of the Bar-Boljare highway has had a key role in raising debt to high levels”

IMF 2018 Article IV

Check the IMF Article IV record

5. Uncertain benefits and opportunity cost

In 2019, IMF staff said the overall project's economic benefits were uncertain and scarce fiscal resources could have alternative productive uses.

“the economic benefits of the project as a whole are uncertain, and the fiscal resources could be put to alternative uses”

IMF 2019 Article IV mission statement

Check the IMF 2019 statement

What complicates the interpretation?

  • The Government's development rationale is evidence of policy purpose, not proof that projected regional outcomes occurred.
  • The IMF GDP estimate was a projection, not an observed post-opening result.
  • The current case lacks a complete post-opening traffic, regional-income, safety, environmental, procurement, and household-distribution dataset.
  • The fiscal-burden evidence is material but cannot alone resolve the overall benefit/burden balance.
