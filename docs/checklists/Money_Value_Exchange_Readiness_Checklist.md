# Money / Value Exchange Readiness Checklist

Use this before publishing, enabling, or promoting any GroundMesh feature that requests, receives, stores, routes, tracks, promises, prices, rewards, exchanges, bridges, or represents money or economic value.

Current posture: no GroundMesh money-flow, token, wallet, fiat bridge, grant program, paid service, exchange, custody path, or Mesh Credit system is live. Existing compute-cycle volunteering is a non-payment capacity signal only.

This checklist is a project readiness gate, not legal advice. A qualified lawyer and accounting/tax reviewer in the relevant jurisdictions must review any real money, crypto-asset, charitable, nonprofit, investment, payment, or value-exchange release.

## 1. Scope And Operator

- The exact feature, public paths, and steward/operator are named.
- The operating person or entity is identified before public use.
- The audience countries and expected participant countries are listed.
- The release says what is active today and what remains only research, prototype, or internal design.
- The release can be paused, reversed, or withdrawn without trapping participant funds, balances, or expectations.

## 2. Value Type

- The release identifies whether it involves donations, grants, sponsorships, payments, credits, points, tokens, vouchers, rewards, wallets, custody, matching, fiat bridge, crypto-assets, accounting entries, or in-kind capacity.
- Volunteer compute, writing, review, translation, hosting, or other in-kind help is not described as payment, investment, compensation, or ownership.
- Any internal score, credit, or contribution marker is clearly separated from money, legal tender, securities, claims on assets, and transferable exchange value unless counsel has approved otherwise.
- No token, wallet, marketplace, exchange, fiat bridge, or public balance ledger is enabled without a separate release record.

## 3. Claims And Expectations

- Public copy creates no expectation of profit.
- Public copy does not imply investment returns, appreciation, passive income, dividends, yield, staking rewards, buybacks, redemption rights, or asset ownership.
- Public copy does not imply charitable deduction, tax exemption, nonprofit status, fiscal sponsorship, or donation receipt unless the exact status is documented.
- Public copy does not condition dignity, membership, essential access, governance rights, aid, visibility, or acceptance on payment or donation.
- Public copy does not pressure people through guilt, urgency, destiny, scarcity, or social ranking.

## 4. Tax, Accounting, And Records

- Recipient, purpose, bank/payment account owner, and accounting category are documented.
- Tax/accounting posture is reviewed before asking the public for funds.
- Donation receipt, invoice, refund/error handling, chargeback, cancellation, and mistaken-payment handling are documented where relevant.
- Retention periods are named for transaction records, receipts, contact details, and support correspondence.
- Public reports aggregate safely and do not expose private donor, recipient, wallet, bank, or transaction details.

## 5. Payments, Custody, And Fraud

- No custody of funds, tokens, private keys, wallets, or payment credentials is accepted unless a hardened operational and legal review is complete.
- Anti-fraud handling is documented, including impersonation, false campaigns, scams, refund abuse, and suspicious transactions.
- Sanctions, AML/KYC, money-transmission, payment-processor, and platform-terms review is complete where relevant.
- Security handling is documented before storing or processing payment data, wallet addresses, receipts, or customer support records.
- Incident response and contact owner are named.

## 6. Securities, Crypto-Assets, And Credits

- If a credit, token, crypto-asset, wallet, transferable unit, fiat bridge, reward, or contribution balance is proposed, counsel reviews securities, commodities, money-transmission, consumer-protection, tax, and crypto-asset rules for the relevant jurisdictions.
- The design does not use phrases like investment, profit, yield, staking, appreciation, guaranteed value, backing, redemption, or passive income unless counsel-approved.
- The system does not let users buy, sell, transfer, redeem, speculate on, or collateralize credits unless a separate approved gate exists.
- The system does not create hidden leverage, debt, interest, penalty, or decay mechanics that affect real assets or essential access.
- Any simulation or fictional fixture is labeled synthetic and cannot be mistaken for a live financial product.

## 7. Data And Privacy

- A data-processing record exists for donor, payer, sponsor, grantor, recipient, contributor, wallet, payment, receipt, tax, accounting, and support data before collection.
- Public and private fields are separated.
- No bank details, payment credentials, tax identifiers, legal names, private addresses, private wallet keys, or sensitive correspondence are committed to the public repo.
- Correction, deletion, withdrawal, and dispute handling are documented.
- Any public ledger is designed to protect personal data and safety.

## 8. Governance And Non-Capture

- The feature cannot let donors, sponsors, funders, token holders, or wealthy participants buy constitutional authority.
- The feature cannot create a public ranking of people by generosity, need, deservingness, reliability, worth, virtue, or loyalty.
- Conflicts of interest are disclosed where relevant.
- Human stewardship remains responsible for release, correction, pause, and rollback decisions.
- The feature preserves voluntary participation and refusal.

## 9. Technical Release

- The release is tested with synthetic data before real use.
- Private storage is outside the public repo.
- Secrets are stored only in approved secret stores, not committed files.
- The exact public paths, scripts, workflows, and data files are listed.
- CI guardrails make accidental public opening visible in review.
- Atlas, status, privacy, legal-readiness, and public navigation are updated only for surfaces actually opened.

## Stop Conditions

Do not open or expand a money/value feature if any of these are true:

- the release asks for public money before recipient, purpose, tax/accounting posture, refund/error handling, and anti-fraud handling are documented
- the release implies charitable deduction, nonprofit status, investment return, asset backing, redemption, guaranteed value, or expectation of profit without counsel-approved documentation
- a token, wallet, fiat bridge, transferable credit, exchange, custody, reward, or balance system is present before legal/accounting review
- the release creates public rankings or access gates based on wealth, donation, payment, need, or contribution
- personal, payment, wallet, tax, bank, or receipt data could enter the public repo
- funds or credits could become hard to return, reverse, correct, or explain
- the release depends on unreviewed CI-generated legal, financial, tax, investment, or accounting conclusions

## Green Definition

This checklist is green only when the exact proposed money/value release has named human stewardship, counsel/accounting review appropriate to its jurisdiction and feature type, documented public claims, privacy handling, anti-fraud handling, rollback path, and passing CI guards.
