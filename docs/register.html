Registration status

GroundMesh registration is real in pilot form, but not globally open.

Human Mesh has a validated public-record foundation, a private participant lifecycle, bounded one-time invitations, and a private rostered-reviewer capability with explicit role acceptance. New intake begins unassigned rather than being pre-owned by one steward. Real multi-steward operation is not claimed until at least two actual humans have explicitly accepted reviewer roles.

H4 global open invitation is still closed. Do not treat this site as a confidential identity intake or emergency registration system, and do not send sensitive identity documents.

What works now

  • Human Mesh H0 declaration and accountability-event contracts with CI guardrails
  • H1 private lifecycle: candidate → review → correction → withdrawal
  • H2 optional one-time invite gate for a bounded reachable cohort
  • H3 private steward roster: only active reviewers with recorded explicit role acceptance can record review decisions
  • H3 synthetic Steward Chair: public training UI that writes nothing and publishes nothing
  • No automatic public declaration or automatic identity verification

Limited invitation

When a steward explicitly activates H2 mode, the same pilot server accepts only valid local invite codes. Codes may expire or be one-use, are stored only as hashes, and are not copied into participant records. A valid invite opens the intake door; it does not guarantee acceptance or publication.

Multi-steward boundary

The H3 machinery can authorize more than one reviewer and preserves which steward made each decision. Being invited or merely added to a file is not consent: reviewer authority requires a recorded explicit acceptance of the current H3 steward-role terms. Unknown, inactive, unaccepted, or non-reviewer identities cannot record a review. Synthetic reviewers prove the mechanism in CI; they do not count as real decentralization. Operational plurality begins only when at least two real humans voluntarily accept the reviewer role.

What is not open

  • Unrestricted planetary registration
  • Automatic acceptance or automatic publication
  • Confidential identity-document intake on the static site
  • Scraping or importing people from external datasets
  • Person scores, reputation ranks, moral labels, or social-credit gates

How expansion stays guarded

ADR-0005 and the Human Mesh rollout remain binding: expansion follows demonstrated handling, not excitement alone. H3 supplies the reviewer-plurality machinery, but H4 universal open invitation waits for real multi-steward capacity, abuse handling, privacy, rollback, and operating experience.
