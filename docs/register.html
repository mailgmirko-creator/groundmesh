Registration status

GroundMesh registration is not broadly open yet.

GroundMesh intends to support voluntary declared joining for any human who chooses it. The long-range architecture is now described as the Human Mesh, but real intake must still begin through careful pilots with moderation, privacy, consent, correction, removal, and rollback gates behind it.

For now, use the current public paths below. Do not treat this site as a confidential intake or emergency registration system, and do not send sensitive identity documents.

Human Mesh Foundation Open Contributors Hub Contact GroundMesh Privacy Atlas

What works right now

  • First contact through the public contact page
  • Low-friction participation through the contributors hub
  • Public map pin requests with explicit consent
  • Builder participation through Get Started
  • A machine-validated Human Mesh public-record foundation, using fictional fixtures only

If you want to signal interest now

Use a minimal first-contact note rather than sending sensitive identity material. A good first message is enough to say your region or country, what kind of participation you are interested in, and whether you prefer public, pseudonymous, or private follow-up.

What is not open yet

  • Broad public registration at planetary scale
  • Confidential or sensitive identity intake on the static docs site
  • Automatic acceptance or automatic identity verification
  • Importing or scraping people from outside datasets
  • Any person score, reputation rank, moral label, or social-credit gate

How it will open

Human Mesh v0.1 defines the long-range public record and accountability-event contracts, but ADR-0005 still controls operations: the first real registration path stays narrow until scope, consent, moderation, operations, and rollback are honestly green.
