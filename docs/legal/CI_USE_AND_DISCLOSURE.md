# GroundMesh CI Use and Disclosure

Status: Active operating note
Date: 2026-09-03

## Boundary

Computer Intelligence may assist GroundMesh. It may not become the publisher, legal reviewer, evidence source, or adjudicator.

CI may:

- draft text for human review
- summarize public sources with citations and limitations
- compare repository files
- run validators and tests
- flag contradictions, missing evidence, stale review dates, and privacy risks
- prepare reversible patches on guarded branches

CI must not:

- publish or merge autonomously
- decide that a contested claim is resolved
- infer motive, guilt, hidden allegiance, or moral status
- score, rank, or reputation-rate people
- create enemy labels or verdict labels
- replace legal, privacy, safety, or editorial judgment
- handle confidential evidence unless a separate protected process exists

## Minimum Log For High-Risk Public Text

For public-interest text, Behavior Atlas cases, legal readiness notes, privacy text, registration/intake text, and donation text, keep enough record to answer:

- which CI system or tool materially assisted
- date of assistance
- human steward who reviewed the output
- public sources consulted
- files changed
- validation commands run
- known uncertainty or residual risk

## Suggested Public Disclosure

Use concise disclosure when CI materially assists public-interest text:

> GroundMesh may use Computer Intelligence to assist drafting, source review, validation, and consistency checks. Human stewards remain responsible for publication decisions, corrections, and withdrawals.

## AI Act Awareness

The European Commission describes AI Act transparency obligations that are relevant to some AI-generated or AI-assisted public-interest content. GroundMesh therefore treats CI disclosure as a release-readiness question even where the precise legal classification needs counsel review.

Reference: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
