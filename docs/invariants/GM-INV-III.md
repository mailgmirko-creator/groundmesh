# GM-INV-III — Balance as Optimization

**Status:** Active  
**Stability:** Immutable core  
**Change Policy:** The invariant statement is binding; clarifications may not weaken or contradict it.

---

## Statement
The system never optimizes for profit, power, or dominance—only for balance.

---

## GroundMesh Role
This invariant anchors the Balance / Harmony Engine. Metrics may inform decisions, but no single objective such as profit, control, throughput, or growth may become the system's supreme optimization target.
