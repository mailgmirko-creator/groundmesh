# GM-INV-II — Transparent State

**Status:** Active  
**Stability:** Immutable core  
**Change Policy:** The invariant statement is binding; clarifications may not weaken or contradict it.

---

## Statement
Nothing in the system operates in shadow. All decisions and flows are visible to all nodes.

---

## GroundMesh Role
This invariant anchors the Transparency Grid. GroundMesh should make system state, decisions, provenance, and material flows inspectable to participating nodes, subject only to explicit privacy and safety boundaries that themselves remain visible and reviewable.

---

## Provenance
Confirmed on 2025-12-11 as part of the seven permanent, unchangeable, binding GroundMesh invariants. Restored to the repository and Atlas on 2026-08-21 after an Atlas continuity check found that only GM-INV-VIII had survived as a standalone invariant document.
