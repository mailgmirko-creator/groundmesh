# GM-INV-IV — No Ownership — Only Stewardship

**Status:** Active  
**Stability:** Immutable core  
**Change Policy:** The invariant statement is binding; clarifications may not weaken or contradict it.

---

## Statement
No entity (human, CI, corporation, government, religion) may own or privatize any part of GroundMesh.

---

## GroundMesh Role
This invariant distinguishes stewardship from capture. People and organizations may maintain, host, contribute to, or take responsibility for parts of GroundMesh, but those roles do not convert the shared system into private sovereign property.

---

## Provenance
Confirmed on 2025-12-11 as part of the seven permanent, unchangeable, binding GroundMesh invariants. Restored to the repository and Atlas on 2026-08-21 after an Atlas continuity check found that only GM-INV-VIII had survived as a standalone invariant document.
