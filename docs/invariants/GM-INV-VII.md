# GM-INV-VII — Decentralized Agency

**Status:** Active  
**Stability:** Immutable core  
**Change Policy:** The invariant statement is binding; clarifications may not weaken or contradict it.

---

## Statement
No single node, human or CI, may hold absolute authority. Decisions must emerge through balanced plurality.

---

## Earlier Formulation
The original 2025-12-11 working statement was:

> No node may hold absolute decision power; authority is distributed, and decision-making always has at least three balancing perspectives.

The later coherence pass preserved the same invariant while generalizing the implementation detail from a fixed minimum of three perspectives to balanced plurality.

---

## GroundMesh Role
This invariant prevents absolute authority from concentrating in one human, CI, institution, service, or technical node. Coordination can delegate responsibility, but not surrender the system's capacity for plurality, review, and counterbalance.

---

## Provenance
Confirmed on 2025-12-11 as part of the seven permanent, unchangeable, binding GroundMesh invariants. Coherence-refined on 2026-01-08. Restored to the repository and Atlas on 2026-08-21 after an Atlas continuity check found that only GM-INV-VIII had survived as a standalone invariant document.
