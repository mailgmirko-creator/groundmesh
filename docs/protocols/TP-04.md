# TP-04 - First Node Grounding

**Status:** Proposed
**Scope:** Human-facing, CI-assisted stewardship
**Related:** TP-03, GM-INV-VIII, GUIDE-STEWARD-LOOP, ADR-0008

---

## Purpose

Keep GroundMesh action rooted in one clear human node before digital work, public decisions, or network expansion.

The first node is the steward's own attention, consent, ordinary life, and ability to choose. If that node is overloaded, rushed, numb, ashamed, or fear-driven, the wider mesh can inherit distortion.

This protocol turns intense insight into one grounded next action without shame, compulsion, diagnosis, or spiritual performance. It is not medical, legal, or spiritual authority.

---

## Core Reading

Overload, craving, fear, fatigue, urgency, and rumination are signals. They are not identity, proof of failure, or commands to obey.

GroundMesh must distinguish:

- insight from integration
- pressure from guidance
- compulsion from willingness
- symbolic intensity from operational readiness
- preparation from avoidance

The test is simple:

> Did one real thing become more real today?

---

## Phase 1 - Stop Feeding The Loop

When the steward notices a harmful, compulsive, or hazy loop, the immediate instruction is not self-judgment.

The instruction is:

1. pause the next repetition
2. move the trigger out of immediate reach where possible
3. drink water or eat something simple if needed
4. breathe slowly for a short interval
5. postpone major decisions

The goal is not to win a dramatic inner trial. The goal is to restore steering.

---

## Phase 2 - Name The Signal

The steward names what is present in plain language:

- fear is moving through me
- fatigue is distorting the frame
- urgency is asking for obedience
- guilt is trying to become a command
- insight is present, but integration is not complete

The wording matters. A signal may be present without becoming king.

---

## Phase 3 - Ask The Steering Question

Before acting, the steward asks:

> What signal is holding the steering right now?

Useful labels:

- fear signal
- guilt signal
- scarcity signal
- avoidance signal
- care signal
- truth signal
- stewardship signal
- repair signal

If the answer is fear, guilt, scarcity, intoxication, overload, or compulsion, the next action must be small, reversible, and body-aware.

---

## Phase 4 - Return To One Real Stone

Choose one grounded action that can be completed without escalation.

Good candidates:

- wash, hydrate, eat, walk, or rest
- clean one small physical area
- write three private lines: feeling, avoidance, next loving action
- update one existing project note
- run one local check
- place one clear task in the next-action list

Avoid:

- public declarations during overload
- irreversible commits or launches while hazy
- converting private pain directly into public doctrine
- expanding architecture to avoid one ordinary task
- treating spiritual intensity as operational proof

---

## Phase 5 - Integrate Before Expanding

After a streak of intense thinking or symbolic work, the next GroundMesh move should usually be integration, not more height.

Integration means:

- simplify the finding
- remove private details
- translate it into shared language
- attach it to an existing Atlas, protocol, glossary, fixture, or checklist
- leave a small trail for the next steward

Little by little is valid when it places real stones. It becomes avoidance when it circles forever without touching the ground.

---

## CI Guidance Boundary

CI may support this protocol by:

- reflecting signals without shaming the person
- recommending pause, water, food, movement, rest, or delay
- separating identity from temporary states
- translating private intensity into safer public language
- suggesting one small reversible next action

CI must not:

- diagnose the person
- claim spiritual authority
- intensify fear, guilt, or grandiosity
- pressure continuity through worth, destiny, or urgency
- encourage harmful substance use or self-harm
- treat overload as consent for major decisions

If immediate harm to a person may occur, the correct next step is human support through local emergency services or a trusted nearby person. Repo automation and CI assistants are not emergency responders.

---

## Completion

The protocol completes when steering returns enough for one ordinary useful action.

No dramatic proof is required. A calmer next step is enough.
